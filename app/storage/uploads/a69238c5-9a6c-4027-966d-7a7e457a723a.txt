1. Laptop and Workstation Replacement
Employees must request manager and IT approval before replacing a company laptop.
Standard laptop replacement cycle is 36 months unless hardware failure occurs.
Company laptops must be returned on the employee's last working day.

2. Industrial Pump & Valve Inspection Procedure
Before inspecting centrifugal pump P-204, operators must isolate electrical power and lock out the breaker.
Visual inspection must verify pressure gauge readings stay between 40 PSI and 65 PSI under normal load.
Bearing housing temperature must not exceed 75 degrees Celsius during continuous operation.
Coolant filter cartridges must be replaced every 500 operating hours.
If abnormal vibration or seal leakage is observed, shut down the pump immediately and log a Tier-1 maintenance ticket.

3. Safety PPE Requirements
Safety goggles and insulated gloves are mandatory inside Zone B compressor rooms.
Acoustic ear protection is required whenever ambient noise exceeds 85 dBA.
