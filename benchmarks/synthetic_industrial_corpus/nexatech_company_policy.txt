1. Leave and Attendance
Every full-time employee receives 20 days of paid annual leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 business days in advance.
The employee's line manager approves leave requests.
Eligible employees receive 26 weeks of maternity leave.

2. Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Remote workers must remain reachable during core hours from 09:30 to 16:00 local time.

3. Information Security and Access Control
Passwords must contain at least 12 characters, including uppercase, lowercase, and numeric characters.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
Employees are not allowed to share their passwords with colleagues or third parties.
Security incidents must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour of discovery.

4. Backup and Recovery
Production servers are backed up every day at 2:00 AM UTC.
Daily backups are retained for 30 days in encrypted local storage.
Backup restoration tests are performed every quarter by the infrastructure team.
